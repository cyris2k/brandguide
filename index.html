§1 — Color

Stay on-palette — always

Use only the exact hex values defined in these palettes. Never invent an off-palette color: no alternate grey, no opacity hacks to fake a new shade. Every color must trace to a token below.

Foundation — warm neutrals

--ink#131010

All text on light. Near-black, warm.

--ink-2#2B2B28

Secondary dark surfaces, strong dividers

--gray#61605A

Muted/secondary text, captions, metadata

--gray-2#AAA79D

Tertiary text, disabled, faint labels

--stone#D1CEC2

Rules and dividers (light); container outlines — used sparingly

--white#FFFFFF

Default light surface. Text sits on plain white.

--canvas#F7F6F3

Warm off-white ground — covers & marketing

Signature accent

--accent#00FF64

Neon green. Main idea OR primary CTA only. At most one per view. Zero is fine when there's no CTA.

The accent earns its power through scarcity. If two elements are neon, neither is the main idea. Restraint reads as premium.

Green ramp — signature hue

Hover over a chip to see its hex. #008038 = eyebrow green on light. #001A0B = deep green for symbol tile and dark-theme grounds.

Secondary spectrum

Six hues × six tones. Default to green. The other five are used equally and sparingly — none becomes a second brand color. Bright tones are never page backgrounds or body text. The one sanctioned home for bright/fluorescent tones is the customer stat card (§7).

§10 — Voice & copy

Direct, sharp, expert, human

Short sentences. Strong verbs. Active voice. Lead with value. Sentence case for headlines.

Direct

Say what you mean. No fluff, no filler. Lead with the value.

Sharp

Short sentences. Strong verbs. Active voice always.

Expert

We know AEO. Use the right terminology confidently.

Human

Warm but never corporate. We're people talking to people.

Copy in practice

Write this

"See exactly where your content gets cited by AI."

Not this

"Leveraging next-generation AI-powered solutions…"

Write this

"AirOps tracks AI citations so you can optimize what actually matters."

Not this

"Our comprehensive, robust platform enables teams to synergize…"

Write this

"You're losing AI visibility. Here's why, and how to fix it."

Not this

"We are excited to announce our groundbreaking new feature…"

Do

Sentence case

"AEO is the new SEO" — not "AEO Is The New SEO"

Short CTA imperatives

"Get a demo" · "Start free" · "See it live" · "Book a call"

Product names — always capitalized

AirOps, Quill, Grids, Brand Kit, AEO, Offsite, Content Engineering

Oxford comma & en dash

ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. Pages 10–20.

Customer role

"Content Engineer" — not "content creator" or "marketer"

Lead with outcomes

Citations, visibility, traffic — not features. Outcomes first.

Don't

Banned words

synergize, robust, comprehensive, seamless, leverage (verb), supercharge, groundbreaking, revolutionary

Em dashes

Use an en dash (–) for ranges and pauses. Avoid em dashes (—).

AI SEO / LLM SEO

Use "AEO" — not "AI SEO" or "LLM SEO"

Passive voice

"Results are delivered" → "We deliver results"

Vague CTAs

"Click here to learn more about our platform"

Positioning language

AirOps owns the Content Engineering and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) categories.

The role

Use "Content Engineer" to describe the role of AirOps customers — never "content creator" or "marketer."

The category

Refer to AI search visibility as "AEO" — Answer Engine Optimization. Never "AI SEO" or "LLM SEO."

The outcome

Lead with citations, visibility, traffic — concrete outcomes. Features are secondary.

§11 — Pre-output checklist

Run before finalizing any visual

Fifteen checkpoints. If any one fails, the output is off-brand.

  • 01Surface is light (#FFFFFF or #F7F6F3 warm alt) unless dark is a deliberate cover/break?
  • 02At most one #00FF64 moment (main idea or primary CTA) — zero is fine without a CTA; nothing else neon?
  • 03One eyebrow on the section opener — Saans Mono Medium, uppercase, green?
  • 04Text is #131010 (light) / #F7F6F3 (dark); neutrals are warm, not green-tinted?
  • 05Containers sharp and sparing (not wrapping running text); accent pill fully rounded; symbol a squircle?
  • 06Headline Serrif (editorial) or Saans Bold (punchy); cover subhead in Saans; editorial lead in Serrif?
  • 07Display figures in Serrif Regular (not Saans Bold), with Saans Mono labels? Buttons in Saans Regular?
  • 08Bulleted lists use a standard disc • (quiet, not green squares); ordered lists use mono numbers?
  • 09Only the provided fonts referenced — no SerrifVF, no Saans Medium, no DM Mono?
  • 10Logo not recolored or distorted; correct light/dark variant; clear space respected?
  • 11Hues default to green? Any cyan/blue/magenta/orange/yellow used sparingly, evenly, never as a second brand color?
  • 12Every color is an exact palette token — no off-palette grey/green/tint, no made-up or opacity-faked shade?
  • 13Stat cards: mode-aware fill; hue combo rotates; a trio is two vibrant + one neutral (neutral visible); upright in decks?
  • 14Canvas filled with consistent module rhythm — no floating or top-loaded dead space?
  • 15No widows or orphans; headlines balanced; measure ≤ ~75 chars; aligned numbers set tabular?
